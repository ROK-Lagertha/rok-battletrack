BATTLETRACK HELP

Player FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about BattleTrack, requirements and rankings.

What is ROK BattleTrack?

BattleTrack is Kingdom 3903's player performance dashboard. Enter your Governor ID to review tracked KvK performance, requirements, historical results and Kingdom ranking data.

Where does my BattleTrack data come from?

BattleTrack uses Kingdom 3903's tracked KvK data. The WebApp reads prepared BattleTrack results and calculations from the Kingdom database; it does not read your game account directly.

What do Regular and Flag Filler mean?

Classification determines which KvK requirements apply to a Governor. Regular players use the configured Regular requirements. Flag Fillers can have a different requirement set based on their Kingdom role.

How are my requirements calculated?

BattleTrack compares your tracked KvK results with the requirement values configured for that KvK and classification. Kill and Dead progress are shown separately, together with an overall progress view.

What do PASS, NOT MET and IN PROGRESS mean?

PASS means the configured requirements were achieved. NOT MET means the completed KvK result did not reach the required threshold. IN PROGRESS is used while the tracked KvK is still ongoing.

What are DKP, KPR, KP and KILLS?

DKP = Deads × 10 + T4 Kills × 5 + T5 Kills × 15. KPR = KvK Kill Points ÷ Start Power and is displayed as a percentage. KP means KvK Kill Points. KILLS means T4 + T5 Kills. You can also use the ⓘ button directly in the ranking panels.

How do ranking ties work?

Governors with the same metric value receive the same rank. The next rank is based on how many Governors have a strictly higher value, so tied players share their position.

What does “Ahead of …% of Governors” mean?

It shows the share of tracked Governors with a strictly lower value in the selected ranking metric. Governors tied with you are not counted as being behind you.

Who is included in the Kingdom Ranking?

All Governors present in the selected KvK's BattleTrack results are included, including Regulars, Flag Fillers, farms and Governors with zero values.

Why can historical values or classifications differ?

BattleTrack evaluates each KvK using the data and requirement context associated with that KvK. Current player status should not automatically be treated as historical status.

Why does Kingdom Progress show only three KvKs?

Kingdom Progress intentionally uses a rolling three-KvK view. When a new KvK is added, the oldest one drops out so the dashboard stays compact and focuses on recent development.
